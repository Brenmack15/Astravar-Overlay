## Implemented on the Convergence draft

The production synchronization client now obtains its campaign handle from GenesisApplicationStorage, waits for readiness before snapshot or mutation, and leaves that shared handle open when a background client closes. The connection inbox, credentials and weapon history keep their existing storage. NotionTransferRepository already used the canonical handle through PR 20. Its reconciliation now normalizes Notion UUID spelling, recognizes renamed physical instances by source identity, and holds same-name matches without stable identity for review. Numbered armor variants retain their distinct identities.

Reforge remains the calculator. The service advertises playableMechanicsVersion 1. Android publishes scoped projected rows only when both ends support them. An existing grant retains its original categories. Actor membership, playable state, item ownership and the current native projection must agree before an equipment change. The transaction advances the Reforge revision, updates the physical equipment and projection, records its native before/after history, and writes child and parent receipts. Recovery validates the child against the historical Reforge snapshots. A same-ID change of payload, mismatched actor role, stale actor/item revision, unknown skill, or an ambiguous shared definition fails the transaction.

The private service remains a mirror and command queue. After APPLIED, callers retrieve the immutable command receipt, read the item and read the actor's calculated statistics separately. The item receipt establishes the item revision. The statistic projection independently establishes the consequence. A historical stale mirror cannot resolve contradictory current source values.

### Wire v1

Use the existing submit_campaign_transaction envelope with campaignId, unique commandId, expectedGrantRevision, player-sourced reason and exactly one native equipment operation. Supply kind playable-equipment, physical recordId, expectedRevision and fields.actorRevision. Actor role defaults to PLAYER. For companions send actorRole COMPANION. For set_equipment_state send exactly one state, attuned or operating value. For edit_equipment_bonus send a bonus with an immutable UUID, label, target ABILITY or SKILL, exact key, amount, enabled, stackingKey and expiresAtGameSeconds. Removal uses removeId and actorRevision. For edit_equipment_defense supply actorRevision and either removeId or a defense containing an immutable id, label, native damageType, kind, nonmagicalOnly, enabled and nullable expiresAtGameSeconds. Active calculator output uses target DAMAGE_DEFENSE, with separate damage type, defense kind and magical restriction in its key. Shared physical definitions need review. Native edits never interpret a descriptive item-property row as an enchantment.

The full failure and permission predicates live in backend/owner-bridge/campaign.mjs and PlayableMechanicsSync.kt. A service rejection, pending delivery, native rejection and commit are distinct outcomes. Restore requires both the ledger children and the Reforge change history. No new Room schema or independent calculator is introduced.
