## Mechanical inputs and evidence

* Treat actor ID, active form, spell grant, item instance, encounter participant, source feature and pool as separate references. Values, labels and source text cannot establish identity. Store actions, attack attempts, batches, casts, rests and later corrections with transaction linkage.
* Mechanically distinguish an approved but uninstalled component, an existing native item and a completed project. Previewing any form, reading a result, inspecting a preset or opening a sheet has no gameplay effect. Unresolved movement, form-dependent breath routes and an enhanced force-only formula remain explicitly unknown.
* Test three forms on one character. The worn transformation focus and carried fallback share the character; removing an active source must suppress or revert according to its configured rule. Verify change in reach, anatomy, movement, ordinary HP preservation and shared dawn/rolled-recharge resources. Test a grounded alternate body independently with a depleted shell, voluntary reversal, duration expiry, eligible blink space, an excluded hearing target and a pending repeat save.
* Require source-qualified typed item/actor review for the historical equipment conflict. Synthetic regression should exercise the original refusal, demonstrate no partial commit, and then validate the newly reviewed operation with its own identity. For an already completed historical progression, an old proposal can only be retired through sufficient same-intent evidence; archive both events without replay.
* Existing official and accepted homebrew rules remain distinct from tentative interpretations and historic draft proposals. Exact full-calculation copy style and modal presentation remain design defaults. Unsupported custom effects should retain their structured evidence and human adjudication status.

## Linked design and historical records

The linked original component records establish several input constraints for isolated fixtures. Optical awareness depends on the scene, line of sight and the type of thermal contrast. It cannot reveal obscured targets behind solid barriers. A short-range teleport is independently bound to its acting character, the shared charge, a Bonus Action and a visible unoccupied destination. Include a passenger refusal and the alternate form’s ground/footprint constraint. A fixed die-substitution item applies only to an eligible attack, recovers on an actual dawn and retains the attack's usual modifiers. Record the substitution as its own method of resolution. A mundane keepsake may already have a ledger identity even when a playable native projection is absent. Resolve that identity before creating any new physical instance.

The independent transformation focus has an owner-bound wearer and a separate retained backup item. A failed or suppressed focus safely restores the original form. Known anatomy, terrain and verified breath configurations constrain the projected actions. No later status update implicitly adds a new biological ability, extra use or attunement slot. The item and progression sources describe different historical times. Retain the earlier owners, prototype development and incomplete-plan records as historical evidence.

The original attack and damage selection references contain assets and both accepted and proposed parameters. Preserve the visuals, source text and locked choices. A source payload substitutes for the selectable component; preserve the fixed components and each separate configured attack. Apply the current per-attack selector rule. A visual candidate for a neutral force benefit remains unapproved. Retain explicit payload level/variant and clear only the temporary selection at its approved execution boundary.

## Canonical mechanics contract v1

The domain uses the command envelope, scoped context and atomic commit from CONTRACT.md. Entity identity is the row ID within the continuity. Each actor owns its own row. Each mechanical entity carries actorId, including the actor’s mechanically configured spell definition, casting grant or item-linked attack. Other actors may use their own grant after a reviewed definition has been provided. Referenced rows must exist in the same continuity under current permissions. Nullable scalar values remain unknown. Time for mechanical eligibility is the explicit in-world clock. Wall-clock timestamps identify recorded events.

| Entity kind | Canonical content |
| --- | --- |
| actor | Role, core profile, level, reviewed proficiency, six base abilities, base AC, HP, ordinary movement, skills, current form, concentration and encounter references, and pool/effect reference IDs. |
| resource_pool | Stable actor-owned pool of ordinary uses, hit dice, spell slots, Integrity, lifetime uses or charges. Includes maximum, current, source, recovery trigger, optional partial recovery amount, Hit Die size or slot level. |

### Source-bound effects and exhaustion

A maximum HP effect carries its physical source and stacking identity. If the actor has a reviewed baseline Constitution modifier, the sheet includes the level-scaled difference from the effective Constitution modifier. The stored ordinary HP, temporary HP and form Integrity change only through their own operations. Attack and spell damage preserve the selected component, type, actual dice and separate flat adjustments.

An optional numerical attack effect declares a canonical pool, exact cost and trigger. The player selects each effect before the attack or cast. An unresolved attack can hold part of the pool for a confirmed hit. Its recorded balance stays intact until the hit resolves. Actual attack attempts debit their attempt costs once. An independently verified miss releases the conditional charge. The owner can review an abandoned attempt and release its outstanding hold while preserving earlier expenditures.

Exhaustion retains a sourced level from zero through six. The legacy profile applies cumulative disadvantages and movement and HP reductions. Its long-rest recovery requires confirmed food and drink. The revised profile adjusts d20 tests and movement by level. Level six blocks ordinary gameplay pending resolution. An unconfigured custom or unknown level requires adjudication. Immunity suppresses the current effects while preserving the recorded level. The six cases in `cloud-structured-effects.test.mjs` verify these contracts over both client protocols.
