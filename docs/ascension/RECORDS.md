## Integration invariants

1. Every behavior begins with continuity, authority epoch and scoped actor/membership checks. Composite keys and foreign keys should reject cross-continuity subjects at write time. Read filters apply to joins, counts, diagnostics, search, receipts, previews and asset access as well as the home screen.
2. Record a command and its item, wallet or project effects once. Link Timeline, item history, encounter history and technical receipts to that event. A read, copy, filter or summary operation has no mechanical side effects.
3. Use one physical item identity, optionally customized from a shared definition. Keep historically installed or combined components available for genealogy without exposing another usable weapon, charge pool or equip slot. Derive access to mechanics from current ownership, equipment, form and attunement state.
4. Represent unknown balances, counts, previous owners, whereabouts and event times explicitly. Sum known contributions with a completeness indicator. Ownership captions alone are insufficient to grant custody or authorize spending.
5. Keep historical source and calculation evidence separate from current values. Reconciliation of the legacy ledger and a playable snapshot needs explicit source proof, including identity, revision, known differences and original receipts. The public worker's current `ANDROID_COMMITTED_RECORD_MIRROR` source designation and device receipt represent pre-cutover behavior.

## Cloud implementation checkpoint

The version-1 discovery registry in `backend/owner-bridge/cloud-records.mjs` exports the contracts for these operations. Android submits the same operation names and argument schemas to the campaign command route. Each staging step goes through the central tenant, epoch, revision, grant, approval and receipt boundary.

| Module | Implemented commands |
|---|---|
| `cloud-holdings.mjs` | Define wallet, transfer exact coins, adjust coins, exchange equal copper equivalent, define or edit shared item description, customize a single physical instance, favorite, acquire, atomically buy or sell, transfer, split, merge, reacquire, equip, attune, change operating state, bind a charge pool, construction payment and source-attested item history |
| `cloud-world.mjs` | Create/edit a known connection, record/update a relationship, add/revise sourced known facts, record a Timeline event or linked correction, define/revise an objective, record a confirmed milestone and save a new checkpoint |
| `cloud-crafting.mjs` | Record/revise research with an optional actual roll, propose/revise a project, link predecessor projects, save versioned blueprints, define/resolve stages, record payments, integrate physical components and link a completed project to an actual existing output |
| `cloud-records-query.mjs` | Party inventory grouped by definition and physical compatibility, per-definition custody distribution, exact party wallet overview, stable paged Timeline, item history spanning stock splits and merges, and bidirectional component genealogy |

All five coins remain separate through payments. The summary exposes both an exact decimal GP display and a known subtotal in copper, alongside unrecorded wallets and unknown denominations. A payment either debits or credits explicit denominations. A coin exchange checks equal value and availability before applying either side. Negative, unknown and overflow amounts fail validation.

Source ownership and physical custody are distinct. External gifts, losses and sales remove the holding from party access. A later confirmed reacquisition uses the same identity. A stack merge retains lineage with an inaccessible absorbed holder. Explicitly portable item attacks, actions, effects and bound charge pools retain their identities and remaining balances through custody changes. Actor-specific learned capabilities keep their original associations. Resolved attacks, rolls and receipts retain the actor who used them. An unresolved shared charge dependency requires reviewed reassignment.

Source-attested historical observations distinguish reported owners, uncertainty, in-world time and publication time. A new source can correct an earlier entry through an explicit link. These entries preserve the current physical holding and wallet. The item timeline also finds component events through their actual donor and resulting item links.

Confirmed material consumption reduces quantity. Explicit whole-item installation retains the original unit and disables independent use. Successor assemblies retain links to earlier components. A stage can declare required source definitions, quantities, integration methods and exact minimum payments. Project completion validates linked stages, recorded material use, paid denominations, predecessor projects and the resulting item's component ancestry. Custom extraction, detachment and inheritance of enchantments require a reviewed mechanical contract.

Actor and category scopes apply to reads, staging and linked endpoints. Aggregate endpoints require party-wide permission. A personal grant can read its actor's wallet and equipment. For the inventory projection an actor ID selects that actor's visible gear. A physical item ID returns that holding and its joined definition. Direct catalog reads, unrelated holdings and party-wide distribution require independent authorization. Item-history and component retrieval enforce archive permission when selecting an archived physical record. Ordinary operations expose player-known information only.

`CloudRecordScreens.kt` provides party item distribution, personal Equipment, wallets and transfers, stack quantity preview, item history, component links, project workflows, known Connections and Chronicle. `CloudRecordViews.kt` constructs read-only queries bound to a specific epoch, revision and grant. Editors use the reviewed cloud command workflow. Offline views use previously authorized local cache records. Semantically resolved custom crafting or advanced mechanical outcomes remain subject to owner or DM adjudication.

The isolated `node --test backend/owner-bridge/cloud-records.test.mjs` suite passes 14 cases against SQLite with foreign keys and atomic batches. They cover exact transfer and exchange with unknown balances, purchase rollback through an injected SQL failure, retry after reopen, scoped reads and permissions, partial sales and reacquisition, stack ancestry, checkpoint recovery, configured attunement, transferable item mechanics and charges, sourced historical corrections, staged costs and multi-generation genealogy. The API 35 Robolectric suite `CloudRecordScreensTest.kt` passed both tests for party distribution, wallet inspection, navigation without unintended mutations, pinned queries and exact wallet display. Release-wide interoperation, physical-device acceptance and signing have their own evidence gates.
