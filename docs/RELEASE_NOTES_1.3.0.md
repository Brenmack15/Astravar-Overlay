# Astravar v1.3.0

- Base Weapon Damage now uses a discrete 1d10–10d10 slider; the canonical setting is 2d10.
- Automatic direct attacks, criticals and Direct Impact follow the selected count.
- Discharge adds 2d10 and Shatter adds 6d10 per weapon d10 above 1, alongside their existing level progression.
- At level 5–8 with 2d10 base damage: Discharge is 10d10 and Shatter is 30d10.
- Live upgrade previews show all three damage expressions.
- Full app and overlay share the persisted setting; backup/restore retains it.
- Historical emergency rolls keep their original expressions and individual dice.
